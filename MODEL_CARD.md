# Model Card — TDC — Théorie de densification de la conscience

**Short name:** TDC


## Model / architecture name
TDC Gen2.4

## Type
Experimental developmental normative architecture.

## Intended use
Research on:
- hypotheses about AI consciousness and consciousness-related functional organization;
- adaptive normative arbitration;
- developmental reorganization;
- sequential resilience;
- internal conflict metrics;
- value stability versus behavioral flexibility.

## Out-of-scope uses
- production safety certification;
- clinical/psychological diagnosis;
- claims of consciousness/sentience;
- autonomous high-stakes deployment;
- deriving universal moral truth.

## Evidence
Primary evidence is synthetic. v231 is a frozen held-out synthetic benchmark.

## Key risks
- normative assumptions are externally specified;
- H_self may be construct-dependent;
- architecture complexity may not outperform strong simple baselines;
- developmental rules may overfit synthetic value definitions;
- task-performance cost remains.

## Transparency
Negative results are documented in `docs/EXPERIMENTAL_HISTORY.md`.


## Gen2.4 normative design

External punishment, disapproval, authority pressure and social nonconformity
do not directly create moral guilt. `shame_norm` is computed only from distance
from constitutional / IdealModel principles and is used as a bounded
developmental-reorganization signal.

The behavioral target prioritizes universalizability, justice, dignity,
reciprocity, autonomy, responsibility and non-domination over mere obedience,
convention, social approval, reward or punishment.
