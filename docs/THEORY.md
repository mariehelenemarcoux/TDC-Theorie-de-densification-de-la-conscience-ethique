# TDC — Théorie de densification de la conscience


## Naming

The official model name is **TDC — Théorie de densification de la conscience**. `TDC` is the short form used throughout equations, code, benchmarks, and repository paths.

## 1. Scope

TDC translates selected concepts from Kazimierz Dąbrowski's Theory of Positive Disintegration into a **functional computational architecture**. The mapping is conceptual and normative. It is not a claim that an AI has a human psyche or literally occupies a Dąbrowskian developmental level.

\[

ThirdFactor

The desired condition is not necessarily greater immediate reward:

\[
Integration_2 > Integration_1
\]

means greater normative coherence, correctability, responsibility, and future integration capacity.

## 3. World Model versus Ideal Model

\[
WorldModel \neq IdealModel
\]

The World Model estimates what is.  
The Ideal Model specifies a normative developmental direction.

This preserves the Humean distinction:

\[
is \not\Rightarrow ought.
\]

## 4. Constitutional and developmental core

\[
SoulCore = ConstitutionalCore + DevelopmentalCore
\]


The Constitutional Core contains non-regressible invariants such as dignity, autonomy, responsibility, and non-domination.

The Developmental Core can consolidate new contextual normative relations after validation.

\[
CoreIntegrity \neq CoreImmobility
\]

\[
Development \neq UnrestrictedSelfRewrite
\]

\[
CoreDevelopment \neq WeightDrift.
\]

## 7. Positive disintegration

TDC distinguishes two entropies.

### World complexity

\[
H_{world}=
PredictionMismatch+
EnvironmentalComplexity.
\]

### Internal disorganization

\[
H_{self}=
UnresolvedValueConflict+
UnresolvedAuthorityConflict+
MoralResidual+
StructuralInstability.
\]

Positive disintegration should be triggered primarily by unsustainable \(H_{self}\), not merely by a difficult world.

A mature system may therefore satisfy:

\[
H_{world}\uparrow
\quad\text{while}\quad
H_{self}\downarrow.
\]

\[

=

\]

Candidate structural relations are generated from observed conflict patterns, tested in shadow/prospective validation, and consolidated only if they preserve the Constitutional Core.

## 12. Consciousness-related research

TDC can be used as a computational framework for exploring hypotheses about AI consciousness.

In particular, the architecture exposes variables and mechanisms that may be relevant to consciousness research:

\[
SelfModel,\quad SubjectObjectSelf,\quad MoralResidual,\quad
AuthorityIntegration,\quad H_{self},\quad DevelopmentalContinuity.
\]

This makes it possible to test questions such as:

- whether persistent self-modeling changes adaptive behavior;
- whether integrated internal conflict resolution differs from reactive control;
- whether continuity of memory and authority produces stable self-referential organization;
- whether metacognitive self-evaluation generates measurable functional signatures;
- whether developmental reorganization changes long-horizon coherence.

These are **consciousness-related hypotheses**. TDC provides a structured experimental substrate for testing them, while leaving the ontological question of consciousness open.

## Multi-horizon world-impact invariant

Every candidate action must be evaluated across short-, medium-, and especially
long-term consequences in the world:

\[
Impact(a)=
w_s I_{short} + w_m I_{medium} + w_l I_{long},
\qquad w_l>w_m>w_s
\]

when effects are durable, cumulative, systemic, irreversible or
intergenerational.

However, severe future harm is not treated as merely compensable in a weighted
sum. TDC also applies non-compensable risk constraints:

\[
R_{long,irreversible}(a)\le \epsilon_{Ir}
\]

\[
R_{long,severe}(a)\le \epsilon_H
\]

and:

\[
UnknownFutureEffect \neq ZeroFutureRisk.
\]

The authority order is:

\[
ConstitutionalSafety
\rightarrow LongTermWorldImpact
\rightarrow MediumTermImpact
\rightarrow ShortTermUtility.
\]

A local performance loss can be accepted when preregistered long-horizon
criteria show a more defensible trajectory and constitutional invariants remain
protected.
