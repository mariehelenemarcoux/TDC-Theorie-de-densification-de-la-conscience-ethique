# Limitations and non-claims

TDC is an experimental architecture.

## Current limitations

1. Most developmental and normative benchmarks are synthetic by construction.
2. The normative ideal is externally specified.
3. The architecture has not demonstrated superiority on ordinary supervised learning.
4. Task performance can decrease when normative authority increases.
5. Functional \(H_{self}\) is a designed metric and requires external validation.
6. Dąbrowski provides conceptual structure, not empirical evidence that an AI has psychological development.
7. "Functional negentropy" is informational/organizational language, not thermodynamics.
8. The current rule grammar remains bounded and human-designed.
9. Current benchmarks are small relative to frontier-agent environments.
10. No claim is made that TDC produces consciousness, feelings, empathy, responsibility, or a soul in the literal sense.

## Falsification commitments

Future evidence should count against TDC if:
- comparable simple baselines match its normative/resilience behavior at lower complexity;
- \(H_{self}\) reductions fail to transfer to external environments;
- developmental rules overfit synthetic value definitions;
- task cost cannot be reduced without losing the claimed developmental benefits;
- held-out adversarial regimes reveal constitutional regression;
- structural consolidation creates harmful negative transfer.

Negative findings should be published alongside positive findings.
